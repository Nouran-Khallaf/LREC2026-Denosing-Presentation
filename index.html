How Much Noise
Can BERT Handle?

Insights from Multilingual Sentence Difficulty Detection

Model reliability has two gates

Sentence difficulty detection can fail before training, because the labels are noisy, and after training, because the model can be overconfident under shift.

Main point: clean the supervision before training, then estimate uncertainty after training.
Motivation

Why denoising matters

Sentence labels are projected from documents. The mismatch creates real problems for style-based classifiers.

1

Projected labels are imperfect

Document-level difficulty does not always match sentence-level difficulty.

2

Style tasks are vulnerable

The model must learn style, syntax, and structure — not just topic words.

3

Transfer amplifies noise

Noisy supervision can hide real multilingual and cross-domain patterns.

Denoising methods

How does each method decide what is noisy?

Each method looks at the same noisy training data from a different angle: embeddings, loss, peer agreement, label flipping, or uncertainty.

GMM

Embedding outliers

Noise appears far from clean embedding clusters.

ST

High loss

Noisy examples remain difficult to fit.

CT

Two models

Each model selects clean examples for the other.

NTM

Label flipping

Noise is treated as class-dependent corruption.

LS

Soft labels

Labels are softened to reduce overconfidence.

GMM

Embedding space separates clean and noisy regions

We embed the sentences using BERT or SBERT. GMM then searches for clusters and flags points that sit in low-confidence or outlying regions.

Optuna: 100 trials tune components, covariance type, tolerance, and threshold.
ST

High-loss examples are treated as suspicious

The model learns clean/easy examples earlier. Examples that keep producing high loss are more likely to be noisy or mismatched.

Final setting: retain the low-loss portion; high-loss examples are filtered.
CT

Two models exchange likely-clean examples

Instead of trusting one model’s own judgement, co-teaching lets two models select low-loss examples for each other.

Dynamic forget rate increases gradually from 0.0 to 0.3.
NTM

Noise is modelled as label corruption

Instead of deleting examples, NTM estimates how often a true label is observed as the wrong label.

Useful when the main problem is systematic class flipping.
LS

Hard labels become less absolute

Label smoothing does not remove examples. It softens the target label so the model does not become too confident on possibly noisy supervision.

Instead of “100% simple / 0% complex”, the target becomes softer, for example “90% simple / 10% complex”.

Take-home messages

What should the audience remember?

Denoising is most valuable when data is small, noisy, and used for cross-lingual transfer.

1

Denoising matters most when supervision is small

English shows the clearest gains because the data is smaller and noisier.

2

BERT is robust, but not magic

Large French training data gives a strong baseline, but noise analysis remains useful.

3

Intersections are interpretable

Agreement between methods gives high-confidence candidates for manual review.
